One data point that may or may not be your case: on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next we traced "a small fraction of responses degenerate with MTP on" to a strided `state_indices_tensor_p[:, 0]` view (the Mamba block table is `1 + num_speculative_blocks` wide once speculation is configured) read with unit stride by a state-carrying kernel, so every prefill after batch row 0 in a prefill-only scheduler step wrote its state into request 0's checkpoint blocks — #55357 has the reproducer and mechanism, #55467 the fix for the PLE short conv. The tell is that the corruption needs two or more prompts prefilled in the same step and disappears when they arrive 150 ms apart, independent of prefix caching. If your fraction tracks concurrent arrivals rather than cache hits, it is worth checking the state-index consumers of the GDN path in your build for the same assumption.
